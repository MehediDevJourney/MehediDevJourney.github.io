How Accounting Logic Helps in Programming

Many people wonder how an Accounting student ends up coding. The truth is, both fields are heavily reliant on systems, logic, and balancing inputs with outputs.

In accounting, if a balance sheet doesn't match, you trace the error step-by-step. In programming, when a bug appears, the debugging process is incredibly similar. The discipline of finding a missing zero in finance translates perfectly to finding a missing semicolon in JavaScript.

MAY 02, 2024

The Era of AI Prompt Engineering

We are living in an era where knowing how to talk to AI is just as important as knowing how to code. AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude are not here to replace us; they are here to amplify our abilities.

By learning advanced Prompt Engineering, I have been able to accelerate my learning process. Instead of getting stuck on a bug for days, I can discuss the logic with an AI, understand the core issue, and implement the solution efficiently.
