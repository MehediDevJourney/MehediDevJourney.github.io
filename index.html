04. Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Why transition from Accounting to Tech?

+

I haven't abandoned accounting! I value the structure and discipline it teaches. However, my deep-seated passion has always been technology. Programming allows me to build tangible solutions and express creativity through logic. I use my financial background to better understand business rules and build software that genuinely solves problems.

What is your primary technology stack?

+

Currently, I focus heavily on mastering the core fundamentals of the web: HTML5, CSS3 (Advanced Grid/Flexbox), and Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+). I believe a strong foundation is crucial before relying on frameworks like React or Vue. I also deeply integrate AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek) into my workflow.

Are you available for freelance work?

+

Yes. While I balance my Honours studies, I maintain a strict schedule that allows me to take on select freelance frontend projects, UI/UX overhauls, and web architecture consulting. Feel free to contact me using the form below to discuss your project.

How do you integrate AI into your workflow?

+

I use Large Language Models (LLMs) as highly intelligent pair-programmers. I utilize them for rapid prototyping, debugging complex logic errors, optimizing CSS structures, and writing boilerplate code, which allows me to focus on the overall architecture and unique business logic of the application.

Do you design websites as well as code them?

+

Absolutely. I have a strong passion for UI/UX architecture. I stay updated with the latest design trends (Glassmorphism, Neumorphism, Bento Grids) and focus on creating intuitive, accessible, and high-conversion user interfaces before I begin the coding phase.
